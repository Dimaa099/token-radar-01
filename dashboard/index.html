Which pre-token protocol is worth farming next?

Every protocol in the snapshot that records no token yet, ranked by what a launch would be worth if its record stayed as it is. The price is not a guess and not a list of names: it is what the market already pays for the tokenized protocols in the same line of work, applied to this one's TVL, growth, runway and reach. Each row carries the farm plan that goes with it: what to do, what it costs, and how long it takes.

"Records no token" is DefiLlama's record, not proof a token does not exist; an implied value is this board's claim about a launch that may never happen.

These protocols already trade, so the board does not rank them: it shows the market's own value for them. They are the comparables the pre-token prices are made from: a category's multiple is the median of these ratios, shrunk toward the global median when the peer count is thin.

How every price on the board is made

One function prices every row. Nothing is priced by name, and no figure comes from outside the snapshot:

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × runway × breadth
  • Category multiple. For each DefiLlama category, take the tokenized protocols in it and their market-cap-to-TVL ratios; the multiple is the median ratio, shrunk toward the global median by the peer count (shrink 6) and clamped between 0.03 and 1.20. A category with fewer than three peers prices at the global median. The table below is the actual pricing table this board ran on.
  • Growth. 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, -60%, +120%) × 0.5: a protocol adding TVL fast is worth more at launch than one bleeding it, and one week's move is capped so it cannot dominate the price.
  • Runway. 0.78 + 0.44 × the square root of its token-free age over four years: the longer a protocol has run with no token, the more usage a launch would capitalise. A protocol with no tracking date is priced at the snapshot's median age.
  • Breadth. +2.5% per chain past the first, up to eight: distribution is worth a premium, and it is the smallest factor on purpose.
  • Who gets ranked. Every watchlist protocol, plus every other protocol that records no token. Above the watchlist floor a candidate must also pass the watchlist's own product screen, so a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades (the snapshot's largest token-free records are exactly those: the wrapped bitcoins and exchange staking wrappers) is never ranked as a launch still to come. Below the floor the net is cast widest, because finding the early ones is the radar's job.

What the board does not do: predict that a token will be issued, or when. A protocol can run for years with no token and never issue one. The farm plans assume the reader wants the position anyway if the protocol's own rewards make it worthwhile, and every plan says never to pay anyone to "claim" an airdrop.
